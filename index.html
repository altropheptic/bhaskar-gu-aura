The problem

Many elderly people take five or more medicines a day. Forgetting is the most common reason doses are missed, and confusion between similar-looking pills leads to double doses. Most reminder apps were never designed for older eyes and hands.

  • 39–59% of missed medication is simple forgetting, across published studies of older adults.
  • 40–50% of elders in high-income countries take five or more medicines at once (polypharmacy).
  • 77% vs 44%: adherence among Indian elders with and without a caregiver involved.

How Dawai Dost solves it

  1. Organise in seconds

    Type a prescription the way a doctor writes it, like Metformin 500mg 1-0-1 after food 30 days, or photograph it. Gemini AI fills in the schedule and you confirm it.

  2. Remind in their language

    At dose time the app rings, shows the pill's colour and shape, and speaks the reminder in Hindi or English with a natural ElevenLabs voice.

  3. Track without mistakes

    One big “I took it” button. Each scheduled dose can only be logged once, so “did I already take it?” always has an answer.

  4. Keep family in the loop

    Share a private, revocable link or a WhatsApp summary so a son or daughter far away can see what was missed today.

Safety and privacy

Dawai Dost is a reminder and tracking tool, not medical advice. If a dose is missed, it never suggests taking two at once; it asks you to check with your pharmacist or doctor. Each browser gets its own private, invisible account; database rules make sure nobody else can read your list. AI keys stay on our server.
