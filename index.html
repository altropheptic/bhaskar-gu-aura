How it works

  1. Add a medicine

    Type it the way the doctor wrote it, like Metformin 500 1-0-1 after food, or photograph the prescription and let Gemini read it.

  2. Get reminded

    At breakfast, lunch or bedtime it chimes and says the medicine's name aloud. One big Taken button logs it.

  3. Keep family in the loop

    Send a WhatsApp update or a private, read-only link that shows what was taken and what was missed today.

Questions families ask

Do I need to create an account?

No. Each browser gets its own private account automatically, with no email or password. The app opens with a sample family so you can see how everything works, and you can edit it or add your own people.

Will it ring if the browser is closed?

Browsers can't promise that, so Dawai Dost also exports every dose to the phone's calendar. Calendar alerts ring even when the app is closed.

Who can see the medicines?

Only this browser. Database rules stop anyone else from reading the list. Family links are read-only, show only today, and can be switched off at any time.

Does it work without internet?

Opening the app and saving need a connection. If the voice or the prescription reader can't be reached, it falls back to a chime, the phone's own voice and the offline 1-0-1 reader.

Is this medical advice?

No. Dawai Dost helps you follow the plan your doctor gave you. For a missed dose it says: don't double up, ask your doctor or pharmacist.
